Our Story

A Source Rooted in Nature

SeJane 硒珍 draws inspiration from the Maofeng Mountain region of Guangzhou, China. According to the brand's source information, the groundwater is drawn from an aquifer approximately 125–200 meters below the surface. We value careful stewardship, clarity of information, and the natural character of our water.

Our Story

Rooted in Nature

SeJane 硒珍 is inspired by the Maofeng Mountain region of Guangzhou, China. Historical brand materials describe naturally occurring selenium and other minerals and trace elements in the source water. We celebrate the natural character of mineral water and the importance of responsible water-source stewardship.

Specific mineral content and standards compliance should be confirmed using current accredited laboratory reports before being presented as verified product claims.

Water Source

Nature's Hidden Reserve

According to historical brand information, the mineral water is drawn from an underground aquifer approximately 125–200 meters below the surface. The brand materials also reference an assessment by Beijing Shanhaitian Evaluation Company regarding the source's characteristics and water yield.

These descriptions are attributed to historical brand materials; independent verification of the depth, assessment, water quality, and current production conditions is pending.
